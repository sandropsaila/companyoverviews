Overview

What PhonePe does

PhonePe's UPI app went live in August 2016 and is the largest UPI player (46.15% of volume in June 2026). It moved its domicile from Singapore to India in 2022 and separated from Flipkart.

SEBI approved its IPO on 20 January 2026. The 100% offer-for-sale was paused in mid-March 2026 and is expected in H2 2026 at a reduced $9–10.5bn valuation versus $12bn in January 2023.

What it sells

Services

Lines per PhonePe's group brochure.

Consumer UPI payments

UPI app in 11 languages; recharges, bills, transfers and co-branded cards.

Merchant payments

SmartSpeaker, Smart Pod, POS, payment gateway and payment links.

Insurance distribution

General, health, motor and life insurance since 2020.

Lending marketplace

Merchant lending and secured loans via partner banks and NBFCs.

Share.Market

Broking and mutual-fund distribution via PhonePe Wealth Broking.

Indus Appstore and ads

Android app store (Feb 2024) and PhonePe Ads; Pincode discontinued.

How it earns

Revenue streams

Payments dominate operating revenue.

FY25 operating revenue ₹7,115 crore

  • Payment services: ₹6,300 crore (88.55%), +31.6%
  • Financial services and other: c. 11.5% (insurance, lending, RBI incentives, broking)
  • Non-operating gains of ₹516 crore took total income to ₹7,631 crore

FY26

  • Operating revenue ₹7,920 crore
  • Payments plus financial services ₹7,619 crore, plus ₹286 crore RBI incentives
  • Total income ₹8,388 crore
