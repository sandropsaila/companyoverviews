Overview

What Futu does

Futu operates Futubull (Hong Kong and China) and moomoo (international), with over 100 licences worldwide. At 31 March 2026 it had 30.17 million registered users, 3.59 million funded accounts and US$155.8bn of client assets.

China operations run through variable-interest-entity contracts (Shenzhen Futu and Haikou Futu). Class B shares are c. 31.7% of capital and c. 90.3% of voting power.
