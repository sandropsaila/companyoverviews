Overview

What PayU does

PayU is the digital payments and lending arm of Prosus. It runs a merchant payments platform that Prosus says accounts for about 25% of India's online payments industry revenue, plus a credit business that lends with bank partners.

PayU's corporate site describes local and cross-border payments and financial services across 50+ emerging markets. The FY26 figures retrieved below are those Prosus reports for its India business, so a consolidated global PayU figure was not found.

What it sells

Services

From Prosus results coverage and PayU's own descriptions.

Merchant payments

Online, offline and cross-border acceptance as an RBI-authorised payment aggregator; UPI and card use cases such as quick commerce and bill payments.

Value-added services and SaaS

About 33% of payments revenue in FY26 came from value-added services and SaaS products.

Credit

Consumer and merchant lending through an asset-light, bank-partnership model; assets under management of $682M.

Payments infrastructure

Wibmo and Mindgate help banks process, per Prosus, one in two UPI transactions and three in four credit-card transactions in India.

How it earns

Revenue streams

FY26 (year to 31 March 2026) revenue split as reported by Prosus.

Payments — $577M (74%)

  • Transaction fees on merchant payments
  • Value-added services and SaaS (33% of payments revenue)
  • Payments EBITDA $12M, four times the prior year

Credit — $204M (26%)

  • Interest and fees on loans originated with bank partners
  • New loan issuances of $221M in FY26
  • Credit adjusted EBITDA $6M versus a $28M loss in FY25
