Overview

What Nubank does

Nu Holdings is a controlled company: David Vélez owns 88.3% of Class B shares, c. 74% of voting power. It reported 131 million customers at end-2025, including 113 million in Brazil (62% of adults).

It received conditional OCC approval for a US national bank charter in January 2026, and has reportedly planned to move its legal domicile from the Cayman Islands to the UK.
