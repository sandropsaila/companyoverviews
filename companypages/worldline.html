Overview

What Worldline does

Worldline is a European payments operator serving merchants and financial institutions: acquiring, payment terminals and e-commerce on one side, issuing and processing services on the other.

FY2025 was a reset year. Revenue fell, goodwill impairments of about €4.7B drove a net loss, management was renewed, and the group is narrowing its scope to Europe. Investigative reporting in June 2025 on high-risk merchants also sent the share price sharply lower.

What it sells

Services

From Worldline's FY2025 results release.

Merchant Services

Acquiring, POS terminals, online acceptance for enterprise and SMB clients; €3,238M of revenue in 2025.

Financial Services

Issuing and acquiring processing, digital banking, account payments; €792M of revenue in 2025.

Mobility & e-Transactional Services

Ticketing and business pay; classified as held for sale (MeTS) and proposed for disposal.

iGaming payments

PaymentIQ platform for online gaming operators, run partly from Malta.

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

Worldline has a Maltese company and office tied to its iGaming payments business.

Worldline Ltd and the St Julian's office

  • Worldline appears on the European Payment Institutions Register for acquiring of payment transactions in Malta.
  • Datocapital lists Worldline Ltd at the Mall Office Complex, The Mall, Malta; WhosWho.mt reports an office in St Julian's launched in 2014 (originally Bambora) to serve iGaming clients.
  • The Maltese entity still appears as DevCode Payments Ltd on the Malta Business Registry and files accounts as a small company (latest April 2025, per WhosWho.mt).
  • WhosWho.mt (June 2025) covered the 'Dirty Payments' allegations against Worldline, which the company disputes, noting its Maltese link.

The two Malta addresses differ between sources; confirm on the Malta Business Registry and the MFSA register.

Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

Deloitte is one of Worldline's two statutory auditors in France.

Deloitte & Associés — joint statutory auditor
