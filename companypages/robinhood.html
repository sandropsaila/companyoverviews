Robinhood

A Menlo Park retail investing and crypto platform offering commission-free brokerage, crypto (including Bitstamp), futures, prediction markets and a Gold subscription across the US, UK and EU.

Overview

What Robinhood does

Robinhood describes itself as a financial super-app. It serves US customers through brokerage, crypto, retirement accounts and a Gold credit card, and UK and EU customers through local entities, adding Bitstamp (acquired June 2025) for global crypto trading.

FY2025 net revenues were $4.47 billion, up 52%, with net income of $1.88 billion.

What it sells

Services

Per the FY2025 Form 10-K.

Brokerage

Commission-free US stocks, ETFs, options, fractional shares, 24-hour market and the Legend trading platform.

Crypto

US crypto via Robinhood Crypto, EU crypto via Robinhood Europe UAB, Bitstamp exchange and Robinhood Wallet (150+ countries).

Robinhood Gold

Paid membership with higher cash-sweep rates, IRA match and the Gold Card.

Credit card & retirement

Gold Card (with Coastal Community Bank) and traditional or Roth IRAs.

Futures & prediction markets

Futures plus CFTC-regulated event contracts; Rothera joint venture with SIG acquired 90% of MIAXdx in January 2026.

UK and EU offerings

UK brokerage, ISAs and GBP wallets; EU Stock Tokens.

How it earns

Revenue streams

Three disclosed revenue lines, FY2025 versus FY2024.

FY2025 split

  • Transaction-based revenues: $2,628M (59%); options $1,123M, crypto $901M, equities $302M, other $302M
  • Net interest revenues: $1,514M (34%)
  • Other revenues: $331M (7%), mainly Gold subscriptions ($179M)

How it earns

  • Rebates from market makers for order flow, plus commissions and fees
  • Margin, cash-sweep, securities-lending and credit-card interest
  • Subscriptions, proxy revenues, listings and IPO offerings
Where it operates

Markets

The United States is the core market, with UK, EU, Singapore and MENA entities.

  • EU via Robinhood Europe UAB (Lithuania), a MiCA CASP passported to 29 EEA states
  • Singapore entity (MAS-regulated) and a MENA entity

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

No Maltese licence or entity, but services are passported to Malta.

Passported, not licensed in Malta

  • No Malta licence, MFSA registration, entity or office was found; the 10-K does not mention Malta.
  • Robinhood Europe UAB's Lithuanian MiCA licence is passported to 29 EEA states including Malta.
  • The June 2026 base prospectus of Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited, approved by Liechtenstein's FMA, was notified to the MFSA and is published on its website.
Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

No Deloitte relationship disclosed; Robinhood's auditor is EY.

No disclosed relationship identified

The FY2025 10-K has no mention of Deloitte and names Ernst & Young as auditor. No advisory, alliance or investor tie was found. Only anecdotal employee forum threads link the names, and they reflect internal independence policies, not a corporate relationship.
