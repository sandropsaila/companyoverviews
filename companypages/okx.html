Overview

What OKX does

OKX runs spot and derivatives trading, a self-custody Web3 wallet, its own Layer-2 network (X Layer) and, increasingly, stablecoin payments and tokenised traditional assets.

Founded by Star Xu as OKCoin in 2013, the business became OKEx and rebranded to OKX in 2022. In March 2026 Intercontinental Exchange, parent of the New York Stock Exchange, made a $200m strategic investment valuing OKX at $25bn. The two have since partnered on perpetual oil futures and a 50-50 US broker-dealer joint venture for tokenised equities. Management says it will not rush a US IPO.

Services

Product lines

Spot & OTC

Order-book spot trading, OTC, trading bots and copy trading; 240+ tokens and 300 pairs for EU users.

Derivatives

Perpetual swaps, futures and options — OKX ranks among the top global derivatives venues. OKX has also acquired a MiFID II-licensed entity in the EU.

OKX Wallet

Self-custody Web3 wallet with DEX aggregation and DeFi access.

X Layer & OKB

Ethereum Layer-2 network using OKB as gas; OKB supply capped at 21m after a 2025 burn.

OKX Pay & Card

Stablecoin spending (USDC, Paxos Global Dollar) in the EU under the Malta payment institution licence.

Earn

Staking and yield products.

Tokenised TradFi

With ICE: perpetual oil futures (May 2026) and a US broker-dealer JV for tokenised equities (Jun 2026).

Strategic stakes

Proposed 20% investment in South Korea's Coinone (2026).

Relationship with Malta

EU hub in Malta

Malta is OKX's single EU licensing base — and the jurisdiction where it has faced the most European regulatory action.

Licences

  • OKX Europe Ltd (formerly Okcoin Europe Ltd) — MiCA CASP authorised by the MFSA on 27 January 2025, among the first in the EU; scope includes trading platform operation, custody, exchange and portfolio management.
  • Payment Institution licence (February 2026) covering OKX Pay and OKX Card.

Enforcement history in Malta

  • FIAU fine €1.054m (April 2025) for AML failures found in a 2023 examination: deficient business risk assessment; no customer risk assessment at onboarding for about half of files reviewed; around $20m of transactions not adequately scrutinised. The FIAU acknowledged 18 months of improvements.
  • MFSA settlement €304k (January 2024), including appointment of an independent reviewer.

Opportunity lens (analysis, not sourced fact)

  • OKX Europe holds a MiCA CASP and a PI licence in Malta — statutory audit, client-asset safeguarding, DORA ICT and AML remediation assurance scope, subject to independence checks.
  • ICE's investment and the tokenised-equity JV raise expectations for institutional-grade controls reporting (ISAE 3402 / SOC 1–2) and assurance-level reserve attestations.
  • The FIAU findings and US plea are material client-acceptance and risk-assessment factors.
