Overview

What Nuvei does

Nuvei describes itself as the infrastructure for every payment, everywhere, covering pay-ins, payouts, acquiring, banking-style services and risk tools through one platform.

It was taken private by Advent in November 2024 and no longer publishes audited accounts. In 2026 it reshaped its leadership team and agreed to buy Payoneer; at close the combined company is expected to have about $3B in annual revenue.

What it sells

Services

From Nuvei's website and press releases.

Pay-ins and global acquiring

Local and cross-border acceptance; local acquiring licences in 50+ countries, including Canada and South Africa.

Payouts, bank transfers and real-time payments

Payout options and account-to-account methods alongside cards.

Alternative payment methods

720+ APMs, authorisation optimisation, currency management and reconciliation.

Risk, fraud and chargebacks

Fraud and risk management, chargeback resolution.

Issuing and banking services

Card issuing, banking services, crypto and digital-asset payments, payment orchestration.

Agentic commerce

Protocol compatibility layer (ACP, AP2, MCP) and Know Your Agent; first in-agent payment with Visa completed.
