Overview

What Kraken does

Kraken spans spot and derivatives trading, custody, payments, staking, prime brokerage and tokenised equities, and owns NinjaTrader, Breakout, CF Benchmarks and Reap.

It raised $800M in November 2025, including $200M from Citadel Securities at a $20bn valuation. Revenue figures are the company's non-GAAP adjusted numbers.

Relationship with Malta

No MFSA licence or Maltese entity found
