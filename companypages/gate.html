Overview

What Gate does

Gate operates spot, margin and derivatives trading across thousands of crypto-assets, plus earn, wallet, payment and — more recently — stock and TradFi products.

The exchange launched in China in 2013 as Bter.com and relaunched as Gate.io in 2017; a 2025 rebrand moved it to Gate.com. Founder Dr. Han (Lin Han) still leads the group. It was an early adopter of 100% proof of reserves and has expanded its regulated footprint sharply since 2025: MiCA and PSD2 licences in Malta, and a separate US arm, Gate US, launched in August 2025.

Services

Product lines

Spot & margin

Order-book trading across one of the widest token catalogues; convert, P2P and block trading.

Derivatives

Perpetual futures, European-style options and ETF-style leveraged tokens (outside MiCA scope in the EU).

Earn & wealth

Simple Earn, staking, dual investment, crypto loans and Gate Wealth private wealth services.

Launch products

Startup token offerings, Launchpool and airdrop programmes.

Payments

Gate Pay, Gate Card and — in the EU — stablecoin payment services under the Malta PI licence.

Web3

Gate Web3 wallet, DEX access and the Gate Layer L2 network around the GT token.

TradFi & stocks

CFDs on gold/FX, US and Hong Kong stock trading; Alpaca partnership (Aug 2026) for US stocks, ETFs and tokenised stocks.

Institutional

VIP, OTC, broker and API programmes.

Relationship with Malta

European base in Malta

Gate describes Malta as its European base; its EU regulated activity runs through one Maltese entity.

Malta footprint

  • Gate Technology Ltd ("Gate Europe") — MiCA CASP authorised by the MFSA on 29 September 2025 (announced 1 October 2025) for exchange and custody services, with passporting across the EEA.
  • PSD2 Payment Institution licence from the MFSA (announced 26 February 2026) for regulated stablecoin payment services in the EU.
  • Gate had a local presence in Malta before MiCA, per its May 2024 anniversary release.

Context: a July 2025 ESMA peer review found the MFSA had only partially met expectations when authorising one (unnamed) CASP. That review pre-dates Gate's authorisation and is not linked to Gate.

Opportunity lens (analysis, not sourced fact)

  • Gate Technology Ltd holds both a MiCA CASP and a PSD2 PI licence in Malta — combined custody, safeguarding, DORA ICT and statutory audit scope, subject to independence checks.
  • Proof of reserves currently rests on a code review rather than an assurance engagement; an ISAE 3000 reserve or controls engagement would materially strengthen it.
  • The group's disclosure history (an alleged undisclosed 2018 breach reported by on-chain investigators) is a client-acceptance and risk-assessment point.
