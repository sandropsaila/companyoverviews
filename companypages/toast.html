Toast

Overview

What Toast does

Toast reports one operating segment with three revenue streams. It has significant operations in the US, Ireland and India but earned no material revenue outside the US in FY2023–FY2025.

Q2 2026 revenue was $1,908M, up from $1,550M a year earlier, with annual recurring revenue of $2.4bn (+25%).

What it sells

Services

Products per the FY2025 10-K and Toast's pages.

Restaurant POS

Android-based terminals and handhelds, kitchen display and multi-location management.

Payment processing

Integrated processing on gross payment volume ($195.1bn trailing 12 months).

SaaS subscriptions

Online ordering, tables, catering, loyalty and back-office, per location.

Toast Capital

Working-capital loans originated by a third-party bank (US only).

Payroll and team tools

Payroll processing (US only).

Hardware & services

Terminals and installation; AI products such as Toast IQ Grow.
