Overview

What Revolut does

Revolut offers personal and business accounts, subscriptions, savings and credit, trading, crypto, insurance and travel services, mainly through its app. It is licensed as a bank in over 30 of its 40 markets and filed a US national bank charter application in March 2026.

FY2025 revenue was $6.0bn (£4.5bn), up 46%, with profit before tax of $2.3bn (£1.7bn), a 38% margin.

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

Revolut serves Malta from Lithuania under its European licence, with no Maltese licence or entity.

Served by passporting; no MFSA licence

  • Maltese customers are served by Revolut Bank UAB (Lithuania), licensed by the ECB and regulated by the Bank of Lithuania.
  • Revolut runs a dedicated Revolut Malta site and Malta business onboarding pages.
  • Malta was one of the first five countries for the RevPoints loyalty launch in 2023.
  • No MFSA registration or Maltese office was found.
