Company profile — Payments platform

Adyen

An Amsterdam-based payments platform with its own banking licence, delivering acquiring, risk, issuing and embedded financial products on a single in-house stack. A Deloitte alliance was announced in 2026.

Overview

What Adyen does

Adyen organises its business into Digital, Unified Commerce and Platforms, delivered on one platform built on its own licences. The 2025 Annual Report adds Intelligent Money Movement and Embedded Financial Products.

FY2025 net revenue was €2,364.2 million (up 18%), with EBITDA of €1,245.7 million (53% margin) and processed volume of €1,394.3 billion.

What it sells

Services

Six service lines per the annual report and website.

Unified Payments

Online and in-person acquiring with a gateway.

In-person payments

POS terminals and omnichannel acceptance; POS volume €310.9bn (22%).

Risk and authentication

Uplift Protect and authentication products.

Issuing

Card issuing for enterprises and platforms.

Platforms and embedded finance

Business accounts, capital and embedded payments.

Intelligent Money Movement

Money-in, management and money-out in one environment (2025).

Auditors

Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

Not the auditor, but a 2026 Deloitte alliance was announced.

Alliance announced, auditor is PwC

  • A Deloitte LinkedIn post from about July 2026 says Adyen and Deloitte are joining forces to help enterprises optimise payments performance and unify omnichannel data.
  • It cites Adyen CCO Roelant Prins and Hans Honig of Deloitte Netherlands.
  • Deloitte does not appear on Adyen's named partner-directory roster, and no investor or client role was found.
  • Adyen's auditor is PwC.
