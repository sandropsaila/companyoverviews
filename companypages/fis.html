Overview

What FIS does

FIS (Fidelity National Information Services, Inc., incorporated in Georgia) supplies deposit and lending core processing, digital banking, card and payments processing, and capital-markets platforms. It says it processes more than 34 billion transactions worldwide.

Structure changed materially in the last two years: in January 2024 it sold 55% of Worldpay Merchant Solutions to GTCR, and on 9 January 2026 it closed the acquisition of Global Payments' Issuer Solutions business (now FIS Total Issuing Solutions) and sold its remaining 45% Worldpay stake.

What it sells

Services

FIS's principal solution lines, per its FY2024 Form 10-K.

Core banking

Deposit and lending core processing, customer management and branch/back-office compliance support.

Digital banking & risk

Mobile and online banking, KYC and account opening, fraud management and ChexSystems decisioning.

Cards & payments

Credit, debit and prepaid issuing and processing, EFT and debit networks; since January 2026 FIS Total Issuing Solutions.

Capital markets

Trading and asset services, commercial lending and treasury and risk platforms.

Wealth & retirement

Trust accounting, recordkeeping and transaction management for banks, insurers and plan administrators.

Item processing & output

Check imaging, mobile deposit capture, print-and-mail and card personalisation.

How it earns

Revenue streams

FIS earns mainly recurring processing and maintenance fees under multi-year contracts, reported in two segments.

Revenue types (FY2025 10-K, Note 2)

  • Transaction processing and services
  • Software maintenance
  • Software licence (point-in-time)
  • Professional services (non-recurring)
  • Other recurring and non-recurring fees
  • A quantitative split by revenue type was not found in sources; the 10-K says the majority is recurring.

Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

Deloitte lists FIS as an alliance partner; the auditor is KPMG.

Alliance partner, not auditor

  • Deloitte's US alliances page lists FIS among its alliance partners.
  • FIS and Deloitte formed a global strategic alliance on IFRS 17 for insurers: FIS updated its Prophet risk suite and Deloitte provided IFRS 17 advice and implementation support.
  • EVP Strategy & M&A Geddes Johnson spent four years in Deloitte's New York audit practice before joining FIS.
  • Deloitte is not FIS's auditor; no investor or client relationship was found.
