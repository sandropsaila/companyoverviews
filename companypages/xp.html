Overview

What XP Inc. does

XP offers financial advice and an open product platform under brands including XP Investimentos, XP Private, Rico and Clear, with c. 18,000 independent financial advisers and c. R$1.5tn in client assets.

It estimates its individual client assets represent nearly 12% of a R$8.6tn Brazilian market.
