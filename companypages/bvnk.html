Overview

What BVNK does

BVNK gives businesses a single API to hold, move and convert value across fiat currencies and stablecoins — payouts, pay-ins, treasury and cross-border settlement.

Founded in 2021 by Jesse Hemson-Struthers, Chris Harmse and Donald Jackson, BVNK grew from a $340m valuation (Series A, 2022) to $750m (Series B, 2024), then drew strategic stakes from Visa Ventures (May 2025) and Citi Ventures (October 2025). After acquisition talks with Coinbase collapsed in November 2025, Mastercard agreed on 17 March 2026 to buy BVNK for up to $1.8bn — $1.5bn upfront plus $300m in earn-outs — and closed on 3 August 2026, ahead of its year-end guidance.

Relationship with Malta

EU licensing base in Malta

All of BVNK's EU regulated activity runs through a single Maltese company.

System Pay Services (Malta) Limited

  • EMI licence from the MFSA — euro payments and e-money.
  • MiCA CASP authorisation from the MFSA (announced 15–16 Feb 2026) covering exchange of crypto-assets for funds or other crypto-assets, custody and administration, and transfer services; passported across the EEA.
  • Combined with direct SEPA access, BVNK describes this as a fully regulated bridge between EUR payments and stablecoin settlement.
  • General Counsel Tina Meschino cited a long-standing positive relationship with the MFSA.

Context: coverage of the licence also notes ESMA's July 2025 peer review, which found the MFSA only partially met expectations in CASP authorisation — a review that pre-dates and does not concern BVNK specifically.
