Overview

What Ripple does

Ripple sells cross-border payments, custody, the RLUSD stablecoin, prime brokerage (Ripple Prime, formerly Hidden Road, acquired for $1.25bn in 2025) and treasury management (Ripple Treasury, built on GTreasury, acquired for about $1bn in October 2025).

Revenue is not published. A CB Insights estimate puts it near $1.3bn a year as of December 2024. A $750 million buyback in March 2026 valued the company at about $50bn.

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

No Maltese licence or entity; services passported into Malta.

Freedom-of-service only

  • No MFSA licence, Maltese entity or office was found, and no Malta-based partners.
  • Ripple Payments Europe S.A. (Luxembourg; CSSF-authorised EMI and MiCA CASP) offers crypto-asset services into Malta on a freedom-of-service basis, alongside other EEA states.
Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

Deloitte attests RLUSD reserves; it is not documented as group auditor.

Reserve attestation

  • Deloitte released an independent attestation on 26 March 2026 covering February 2026: $1.61bn of reserves against 1.53bn RLUSD at 19 February, and $1.56bn against 1.49bn at 27 February.
  • The work was done under AICPA attestation standards and covered custodian Standard Custody Trust Company, LLC.
  • Deloitte is not documented as Ripple's financial auditor, adviser, alliance partner or investor.
