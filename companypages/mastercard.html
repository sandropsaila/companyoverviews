Company profile — Card network & payments

Mastercard

A global payments technology company running a card network in more than 220 countries and territories. Like Visa, it is not a bank and does not issue cards or extend credit.

Overview

What Mastercard does

Mastercard switches transactions between issuers and acquirers worldwide, covering more than 150 currencies, and guarantees settlement of many of those transactions. It also sells value-added services, disbursement and remittance products, and commercial and government solutions.

FY2025 net revenue was $32.8 billion (up 16%), with net income of $15.0 billion and gross dollar volume of $10.6 trillion.

What it sells

Services

Six core offerings per the FY2025 Form 10-K.

Payment network

Switching of domestic and cross-border transactions between issuers and acquirers.

Value-added services

Security, digital and authentication (including tokenisation), engagement, processing and gateway services.

Mastercard Move

Disbursements and remittances to 17+ billion endpoints.

Real-time account-based payments

ACH batch and real-time clearing and settlement infrastructure.

Commercial & small business

Corporate, procurement and fleet cards; Smart Data; virtual cards.

Government solutions

Digital revenue collection, procurement and data insights.

How it earns

Revenue streams

Mastercard reports net revenue (after customer rebates and incentives) in two categories.

FY2025 net revenue split

  • Payment network: $19,476M (+12%), about 59%
  • Value-added services and solutions: $13,315M (+23%), about 41%
  • Total: $32,791M

How fees arise

  • Payment network: fees on gross dollar volume plus switching and assessments
  • Value-added services: security, authentication, engagement, processing, insights and ACH/real-time payments
  • Rebates and incentives are a meaningful deduction inside the network line

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

A partnership with FinanceMalta exists, but no Malta office or MFSA licence was found.

Partnership with FinanceMalta; no licence found

  • In July 2023 Mastercard partnered with FinanceMalta on a study with recommendations to develop Malta's fintech and payments ecosystem.
  • A Mastercard Malta site exists in English, and the Partner Advantage form accepts Malta-based participants.
  • No Malta office, branch or legal entity appears in Mastercard's locations or careers listings.
  • No MFSA licence was disclosed; the register's licence view returned an error, so a definitive negative could not be confirmed.
Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

No relationship found; Mastercard's auditor is PwC.

No disclosed relationship identified

Deloitte is not Mastercard's auditor (PwC since 1989) and does not appear in the FY2025 10-K. Neither Deloitte's alliance pages nor Mastercard's partner directory list the other, and no press release or filing shows an advisory, alliance or investor tie.
