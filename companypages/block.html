Overview

What Block does

Block has two reportable segments, Square (commerce ecosystem for sellers) and Cash App (consumer finance, including Afterpay BNPL), plus a bitcoin ecosystem that includes Proto, Spiral and Bitkey and the TIDAL music service.

FY2025 net revenue was $24.19 billion, flat on 2024 ($24.12 billion) as lower bitcoin revenue offset growth in commerce enablement and financial solutions.

What it sells

Services

Business lines per the FY2025 Form 10-K.

Square

Card acceptance, point of sale and software, lending, Square Card and checking; 4M+ sellers and $228bn GPV in 2024.

Cash App

P2P payments, Cash App Card, investing, Borrow and savings; 57M monthly actives in the US (Dec 2024).

Afterpay

Buy-now-pay-later, reported inside the Cash App segment.

TIDAL

Music streaming and artist tools.

Bitkey

Self-custody bitcoin wallet with a hardware device.

Proto and Spiral

Bitcoin mining systems and open-source bitcoin development.
