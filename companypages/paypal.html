Company profile — Payments

PayPal

A San Jose payments company with 439 million active accounts across about 200 markets, spanning PayPal, Venmo, Braintree, buy-now-pay-later and crypto. Unlike most peers, it has a documented Deloitte consulting relationship.

Overview

What PayPal does

PayPal operates a two-sided network linking consumers and merchants. Its offerings include digital wallets and P2P (PayPal and Venmo), branded checkout, Braintree processing, BNPL and credit, in-person commerce, and crypto.

FY2025 net revenues were $33.2 billion, up 4.3%. Enrique Lores became CEO on 1 March 2026, succeeding Alex Chriss.

What it sells

Services

Principal services per the FY2025 Form 10-K.

Wallets and P2P

PayPal and Venmo for shopping, saving, crypto and sending money.

Branded checkout

PayPal and Venmo checkout for enterprises and SMBs.

Braintree

Unbranded online payment processing for enterprises and platforms.

BNPL and credit

Pay Later, consumer credit and debit products, and business financing.

In-person commerce

Point-of-sale, payouts and risk tools.

Crypto and value-added

Crypto buy and sell; credit, referral, subscription and gateway services.

How it earns

Revenue streams

Two reported categories; percentages calculated from disclosed amounts.

FY2025 split ($M)

What is in each

  • Transaction: net fees on payment volume plus currency conversion, cross-border, instant-transfer and crypto fees
  • Value-added: partnerships, interest and fees on credit products, interest on customer balances, referral and subscription fees

Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

Deloitte is a disclosed consulting and tax vendor, not the auditor.

Consulting and tax services

  • PayPal's own third-parties list names Deloitte Consulting Inc. (USA) and Deloitte GmbH Wirtschaftsprüfungsgesellschaft / Deloitte LLP (Germany) for financial consulting support services.
  • Deloitte Tax LLP (US, UK, Luxembourg) is named as a compliance partner reviewing account information for FATCA reporting to Luxembourg authorities.
  • Deloitte is not PayPal's auditor (PwC since 2000).
  • No technology alliance or investment relationship was found.
