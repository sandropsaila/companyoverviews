Overview

What Trumia does

Trumia Limited is a fully licensed Electronic Money Institution based in Malta, positioning itself as a "new-generation" payment services provider for businesses and individuals in sectors that often struggle with mainstream banking access — explicitly naming e-commerce, travel and online gaming as beneficiaries of its multi-currency account and cross-border payment offering. The company describes itself as built around identifying each client's specific financial needs and delivering tailored, secure technology solutions with an emphasis on customer service.

Trumia Limited (company number C96757, LEI 984500BD9ACB39C97131) was authorised by the Malta Financial Services Authority as an e-money institution on 29 May 2023, under reference TRUM. It soft-launched publicly in May 2024. The company's ownership structure is not publicly disclosed, and — reflecting its recent founding — it does not yet have a lengthy public financial or regulatory track record.

What it sells

Services

Trumia's product range centres on multi-currency accounts and cross-border payment rails for both corporate and individual clients.

Multi-currency accounts

Single- and multi-currency operational and settlement accounts for corporate clients, with local IBANs across supported currencies.

Foreign exchange

Automated currency exchange for both natural persons and companies, marketed as fast and low-friction.

SEPA & SWIFT payments

Participation in SEPA (Credit Transfer, Instant Credit Transfer, Direct Debit and Direct Debit B2B) and the SWIFT network (BIC: TRUMMTM2XXX), including readiness for Verification of Payee.

Internet & mobile banking

Digital-first account management for corporate clients across the EU and EEA, without a traditional bank-branch model.

Consumer wallets

Personal e-money accounts for individuals, alongside the corporate account offering.

Trumia Ecosystem (P2P)

Peer-to-peer payments between Trumia account holders, marketed as fast and cost-effective compared with external transfers.

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

Trumia is not a foreign company with a Maltese branch — it is a Maltese company through and through, licensed, headquartered and embedded in the island's fintech community.

A genuinely Malta-native institution

  • Trumia Limited is incorporated in Malta (company no. C96757) with its registered office at Quad Central, Birkirkara — the address it uses for all regulatory and public-facing purposes.
  • Its sole regulatory authorisation — as an Electronic Money Institution — comes directly from the Malta Financial Services Authority, granted in May 2023.
  • Trumia is a listed member of the Financial Institutions Malta Association (FIMA), named alongside other Malta-based fintechs profiled in this index, including Bitpanda and Trust Payments, as part of what FIMA describes as Malta's fintech community "shaping Malta's future as a global hub for financial services."
  • CEO Mark Curmi sits on FIMA's board, giving Trumia a direct voice in Malta's fintech-industry association alongside his executive role.

Of all the companies in this index, Trumia's relationship with Malta is among the most complete: unlike multinational groups that merely hold a Maltese licence or office, Trumia's entire regulatory identity, leadership network and industry association membership are Maltese by origin — closer in kind to Paytently's home-grown Malta presence than to a foreign entrant's local branch.
