Overview

What Kaspi.kz does

Kaspi.kz runs three platforms through one app: Payments, Marketplace and Fintech. Founders Kim and Lomtadze own c. 20.8% and 22.6%; the four principal holders together own 66.5%.

In 2025 it consolidated Türkiye's Hepsiburada (Marketplace segment) and plans to invest c. $300M on closing the acquisition of Rabobank A.Ş. Its original IPO was in London; the ADSs are now on Nasdaq.

What it sells

Services

Platforms per Kaspi's investor-relations pages and 20-F.

Payments

Kaspi Gold card and wallet, Kaspi Pay QR (71% of FY2024 TPV), bills, P2P and B2B payments.

Marketplace

E-commerce, e-grocery, Kolesa.kz cars, travel and delivery (99m deliveries in FY2024).

Fintech

Deposits, BNPL (41% of FY2025 financing), loans and merchant finance.

GovTech

Digital documents, licences, registrations and tax payment.

Kaspi Bank

Licensed bank behind the group's accounts and payments.
