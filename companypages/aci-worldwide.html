Overview

What ACI Worldwide does

ACI sells software that runs payments for banks, merchants and billers. In 2025 it merged its bank and merchant units into one Payment Software segment and kept the Biller segment, which runs Speedpay.

FY2025 revenue was $1.76B (+10%), its second straight year of double-digit growth, with 71% of nine-month revenue recurring.
