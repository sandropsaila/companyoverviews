Company profile — Payments & Fintech

Trustly

A Stockholm-based open banking payments group operating a global "Pay by Bank" (account-to-account) platform, connecting merchants directly to consumers' online bank accounts.

Overview

What Trustly does

Trustly builds account-to-account ("A2A") payment infrastructure that lets a consumer pay a merchant directly from their online bank account — no card, app download or account creation required. The group has grown through organic expansion and acquisition (PayWithMyBank in the US, Ecospend in the UK, SlimPay in continental Europe) into a platform spanning deposits, payouts, recurring billing and data-driven consumer recognition.

The corporate group is structured beneath Trustly AB (publ), the Swedish top holding company, which owns Trustly Holding AB (publ) (the entity that issues public debt and consolidated financial statements) and, beneath it, the principal regulated operating company, Trustly Group AB — an authorised Swedish payment institution supervised by the Swedish Financial Supervisory Authority (Finansinspektionen). Nordic Capital has been the group's controlling investor since 2018, alongside institutional co-investors including BlackRock and Neuberger Berman.

What it sells

Services

Trustly's product suite covers the full payment lifecycle for merchants, from taking a payment to recognising the same consumer on a repeat visit.

Pay by Bank (deposits)

Instant account-to-account payment initiation at checkout, authenticated through the consumer's own online bank login.

Payouts & refunds

Real-time disbursement of winnings, refunds and payroll-style payouts back to a consumer's bank account.

Recurring payments

Subscription and direct-debit style billing, built out through the 2023 combination with SlimPay, for utilities, insurance and subscription merchants.

Trustly ID

A biometric, bank-grade identity and login solution, launched to speed up onboarding for gaming and financial-services partners.

Trustly Insights / Azura

A proprietary data engine that recognises consumers across merchants and banks, used by merchants to lift conversion and lifetime value.

Pay N Play & Scan & Pay

Instant-onboarding deposit/withdrawal flows built for online casino operators, plus a cashless, QR-based funding flow for physical gaming machines in the US.

How it earns

Revenue streams

Trustly is a transaction-fee business: it earns primarily by taking a fee each time it moves money on a merchant's behalf, rather than through subscription software licences.

Core streams

  • Transaction fees on pay-ins (consumer deposits to merchants)
  • Transaction fees on payouts and refunds
  • Recurring-payment / subscription-billing fees (via the SlimPay platform)
  • Data and insight services sold to merchants (Trustly Insights)

Reporting basis

  • Revenue is recognised per completed transaction — each pay-in or pay-out is treated as a separate performance obligation
  • Reported externally by two operating segments: Europe and Americas
  • Volume is tracked via Total Payment Value ("TPV") — the value of transactions processed, which drives fee revenue

In FY2025, Trustly Holding Group revenue split was roughly SEK 1,357.9m from the Americas segment and SEK 1,069.8m from Europe.

Where it operates

Markets

Trustly reports its business across two operating segments — Europe and the Americas — connecting merchants to consumers through roughly 12,000 partner banks in more than 30 countries.

Europe

  • Sweden (home market, licensed by Finansinspektionen)
  • United Kingdom (via Trustly UK Ltd. and the Ecospend acquisition; FCA-regulated)
  • France (via SlimPay; ACPR-regulated)
  • Germany, Netherlands, Spain, Poland, Denmark, Norway, Finland

Americas & other

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

Malta is one of Trustly's established European operating locations, anchored by two locally registered subsidiaries and a long-standing office in Sliema.

Corporate presence

  • Trustly Malta Holding Limited (registration no. C 49649), incorporated in 2010, held by Trustly Group AB.
  • Trustly Malta Ltd. (registration no. C 49689), a related Malta-registered operating entity.
  • Office historically located at the Tagliaferro Business Centre, Sliema — one of Trustly's listed international locations alongside its London office.

Malta's significance to Trustly is closely tied to the island's position as a global hub for online gaming: the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) licenses a large share of the world's online casino and betting operators, and Trustly's iGaming-focused products — including Pay N Play instant deposit/withdrawal and Trustly ID — are widely used by MGA-licensed operators to fund play and verify players. Trustly itself is not an MGA licensee; its payment institution licences are held in Sweden (Finansinspektionen), the UK (FCA) and France (ACPR), with its Maltese entities providing local corporate presence for the group's business with Malta-based gaming operators rather than an independent gaming licence.

Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

Statutory auditor of the ultimate parent

  • Deloitte AB is registered with Bolagsverket (the Swedish Companies Registration Office) as the statutory auditor of Trustly AB (publ), the group's top Swedish holding company.
  • The signing (huvudansvarig) auditor on that engagement is Patrick Olof Honeth.
  • This sits alongside — rather than replaces — Ernst & Young's role auditing the group's main reporting and operating subsidiaries (see Auditors section).

Beyond this statutory audit appointment at the parent-company level, the public sources reviewed for this profile did not identify a distinct Deloitte Malta engagement with Trustly's Maltese entities, nor any other disclosed advisory relationship between Deloitte and Trustly. Any such engagement, if it exists, would sit outside the scope of what is publicly disclosed in company registries and investor materials.
