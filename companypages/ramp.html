Overview

What Ramp does

Ramp Business Corporation (Delaware) describes itself as an AI-native finance platform. Annualised revenue passed $1bn in October 2025 and $1.5bn in June 2026, per Bloomberg as reported; Ramp declined to comment on the October round.

In March 2026 it acquired Billhop (UK and Sweden payment licences) and opened offices in London and Stockholm. In June 2026 Karim Atiyeh became co-CEO alongside Eric Glyman.

What it sells

Services

Product set per Ramp's own pages.

Corporate cards

Visa charge cards with spend controls and 1.5% cash back.

Expense management

Automated expense reporting and reimbursements.

Bill Pay / AP

AI-driven invoice capture, approvals and payments via ACH, wire, cheque or international rails.

Travel

Policy-based booking with virtual cards.

Banking & treasury

Unlimited international wires and yield on balances.

AI and accounting tools

AI spend visibility (April 2026) and Stack for accounting firms (June 2026).
