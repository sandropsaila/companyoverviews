Overview

What Andaria does

Andaria provides regulated payment accounts, card issuing and embedded-finance infrastructure to businesses, with particular focus on sectors such as sports, aviation, marinas, maritime, travel, memberships and iGaming. It operates through two licensed entities so that it can serve both EEA and UK clients post-Brexit.

Andaria Financial Services Limited (Malta, company no. C 97170) was authorised by the MFSA as a financial institution/EMI on 19 May 2021 (reference AFSL10) with an EEA passport (BIC ANFVMTMM; LEI 213800ZESLEC74IL4Y76). Andaria Financial Services UK Limited (company no. 12840774) was authorised by the FCA on 11 October 2021 (reference 933187; BIC ANIEGB22XXX; LEI 213800ZDX7ZWPH8IEV51) and is owned by Andaria Capital Limited.

What it sells

Services

A B2B-focused stack spanning accounts, cards, embedded finance, payee verification and white-labelled crypto.

Business payment accounts

Multi-currency accounts with virtual IBANs (vIBANs), supporting SEPA, SWIFT and UK Faster Payments across roughly 19–25 currencies.

Embedded finance

White-label accounts and cards for platforms, with KYC and transaction monitoring built in.

Card programmes

Principal member of Mastercard and Discover (2024) and Visa (2025); PCI DSS certified (2024).

Andaria Crypto

White-labelled crypto services powered and operated by YouHodler Italy S.r.l. (OAM VASP no. PSV 58). Andaria itself is not a CASP/VASP.

Verification of Payee

Payee-name verification via a partnership with tell.money (announced August 2025).

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

Andaria is Malta-native: headquartered, licensed and led from the island.

Malta at the core

  • Headquartered in Santa Venera, Malta; Andaria Financial Services Limited is registered in Malta (C 97170).
  • Licensed by the MFSA as a financial institution under Article 5 of the Financial Institutions Act (ref. AFSL10, 19 May 2021).
  • The Malta entity is the EU-facing arm, passporting services to EEA customers after Brexit; the UK entity serves UK clients.
  • Malta CEO Kurt Azzopardi and several C-suite/board members are Malta-based.

Like Trumia and Paytently elsewhere in this index, Andaria's regulatory identity is Maltese by origin rather than a foreign entrant's local branch.
