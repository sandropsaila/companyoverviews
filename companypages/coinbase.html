Overview

What Coinbase does

Coinbase offers crypto trading for consumers and institutions, Coinbase One subscriptions, custody and prime brokerage, staking, USDC-related stablecoin products, and the Base network and Developer Platform. It added Deribit in 2025 to strengthen derivatives.

FY2025 total revenue was $7.18 billion (up 9%) and net income $1.3 billion, down from $2.6 billion in 2024. Revenue is heavily US-weighted ($6.0bn US versus $1.2bn international).

What it sells

Services

Core services per Coinbase's product catalogue and FY2025 Form 10-K.

Trading

Retail and institutional spot, derivatives and international perpetuals, prediction markets and 24/5 US stock trading.

Coinbase One

Subscription with reduced fees, USDC rewards and boosted staking; three tiers and a credit card.

Custody and Prime

Cold-storage custody and institutional prime brokerage and financing.

Staking and earn

Blockchain rewards, USDC rewards, crypto-backed borrowing and cards.

Stablecoins and payments

USDC reserve economics, custom stablecoins and stablecoin payments.

Base and developer platform

Base layer-2, self-custodial wallets, onramp and exchange APIs.

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

No Coinbase relationship with Malta was found.

No Maltese licence or entity found

  • No MFSA licence appeared in the register pages retrieved, and the 10-K's list of licences names no Malta authorisation.
  • Coinbase's EEA MiCA licence is Luxembourg's; a June 2025 Reuters report put Luxembourg as its EU base, while Gemini was reported close to an MFSA licence.
  • Malta had earlier licensed rivals OKX and Crypto.com (January 2025), and ESMA scrutinised the pace of approvals.
Professional-services relationship

Relationship with Deloitte

Deloitte is Coinbase's auditor.

Auditor relationship confirmed

  • Deloitte & Touche LLP has audited Coinbase Global, Inc. since 2020 and audits internal control over financial reporting.
  • Shareholders ratified Deloitte for FY2024 at the 2024 annual meeting.
  • No advisory engagement, investment or alliance beyond the audit was found, and Deloitte's alliance pages do not list Coinbase.
