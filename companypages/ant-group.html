Company profile — Digital payments & finance

Ant Group

The Hangzhou-based operator of Alipay and an affiliate of Alibaba, with payments, digital finance, technology and international (Ant International) businesses. Private and unlisted, with no consolidated revenue published since its suspended 2020 IPO.

Overview

What Ant Group does

Ant Group runs Alipay and a platform that supports more than 2,000 financial institutions in China. Its pillars are digital payment, digital finance (Huabei, Jiebei, insurance and wealth platforms), technologies (AntChain, OceanBase, Zoloz) and globalisation through Ant International (Alipay+, Antom, WorldFirst).

In September 2026 Ant merged its payment, Alipay and Zhima Credit units into a single Alipay Business Group, cutting its tier-one business groups from five to four. The planned October 2020 dual listing (about US$34.5bn raise) was suspended by regulators and has not been revived.

What it sells

Services

Five pillars per Ant's own profile.

Digital payment (Alipay)

China's leading digital payment platform with 1bn+ users and 80M merchants.

Digital finance

Tech services to 2,000+ financial institutions; Ant Insurance, Ant Fortune, Huabei and Jiebei; associate MYbank has served 50M+ SMEs.

Digital technologies

AntChain blockchain, OceanBase database, Zoloz biometrics, SOFAStack and mPaaS.

Ant International

Alipay+ (36 wallets, 1.7bn accounts), Antom, WorldFirst and embedded finance; ANEXT Bank in Singapore.

Digital connectivity

Open platform for 12,000+ ISVs built on Alipay mini-programs and AI agents.

Digital health

Medical insurance codes and the AQ AI health app reaching nearly 900M users.

How it earns

Revenue streams

Technology-service and platform fees rather than balance-sheet lending. No post-2020 segment split has been published.

FY2019 split (listing document)

  • Digital payment and merchant services: RMB 51,905M (43.0%)
  • CreditTech: RMB 41,885M (34.7%)
  • InvestmentTech: RMB 16,952M (14.1%)
  • InsureTech: RMB 8,947M (7.4%)
  • Innovation and other: RMB 930M (0.8%)

How fees arise

  • CreditTech: fees from partner banks on credit balances originated (about 98% underwritten by partners or securitised)
  • InvestmentTech: fees on volume of products distributed for partner asset managers
  • InsureTech: a percentage of premiums generated for partner insurers
