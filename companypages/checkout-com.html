Company profile — Payments, UK

Checkout.com

Private London-based payments acquirer valued at $12bn in September 2025, audited by PwC, with no Maltese licence (EU access is passported from France).

Founded
2009 as Opus Payments; Checkout.com from 2012 (Guillaume Pousaz)
Headquarters
Wenlock Works, London N1
Ownership
Private; Pousaz is person with significant control
Valuation
$12bn (Sept 2025 employee buyback)
Footprint
56 countries, 10 acquiring licences, c. 2,300 staff
Overview

What Checkout.com does

Checkout.com says net revenue grew more than 30% in 2025, and it expects $750M of annualised net revenue for 2026 and c. $150M of adjusted EBITDA. It processed $300bn in 2025 and expects $480bn in 2026.

UK filings are standalone Checkout Ltd accounts, not consolidated group accounts, so group revenue figures come from company announcements.

What it sells

Services

Service lines per Checkout.com's own site.

Acquiring

Card, local and alternative payment methods across a global network.

Online acceptance

Unified Payments API, Flow, hosted pages, SDKs and payment links.

Issuing

Card programmes for merchants.

Payouts

Pay-outs to bank accounts and cards.

Fraud, risk and compliance

Fraud detection, 3DS, identity verification, disputes and vault.

Performance and treasury

Intelligent Acceptance, network tokens, Treasury & FX and a business account (2024).

How it earns

Revenue streams

Transaction-based processing; ~77% of gross revenue is passed through as scheme and interchange cost.

Relationship with Malta

Malta is reached only through passporting.

Passported market, no Maltese licence

  • Checkout.com lists no Maltese entity or MFSA authorisation.
  • Checkout SAS's French ACPR e-money licence (no. 17208) is passported to all EEA states, including Malta.
  • None of its 20 offices is in Malta.
