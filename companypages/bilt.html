Overview

What Bilt does

Bilt lets renters earn points on rent. Its July 2025 update says its network covers about 5 million homes, roughly 1 in 4 US apartment buildings, with 5 million+ members.

In February 2026 it relaunched its card as Bilt Card 2.0, ending the Wells Fargo partnership; new cards are issued by Column N.A. and serviced by Cardless. Bilt says it expects to cross $1bn in revenue by Q1 2026.

What it sells

Services

Services per Bilt's business update and newsroom.

Rent and housing payments

Bilt Alliance: points on rent via the app or landlord portals (Yardi, RealPage, Entrata).

Co-branded card

Bilt Mastercard (Wells Fargo until 2026), now Bilt Card 2.0 in three tiers (no-fee, $95, $495).

Neighbourhood commerce

c. 40,000–50,000 merchant locations earning points.

Points redemption

Travel, 1:1 transfers to airline and hotel partners, dining, fitness and housing costs.

Credit building and expansion

Rent reporting to bureaus, Venmo rent payments, mortgage rewards with UWM, student housing.
