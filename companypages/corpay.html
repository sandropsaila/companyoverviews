Overview

What Corpay does

Corpay organises its business as Corporate Payments, Vehicle Payments and Lodging Payments, serving more than 800,000 business clients.

It completed the acquisition of Alpha Group International plc for an estimated $2.4bn in 2025, its largest cross-border deal. Alpha's Malta entity is the group's route to MFSA regulation.

Regional relationship

Relationship with Malta

Corpay's Alpha FX Europe Limited is MFSA-authorised in Malta.

Alpha FX Europe Limited (C 96623)

  • Corpay's compliance page lists Alpha FX Europe Limited as a Corpay-branded entity covering Malta and the EEA for FX, global payments and currency risk.
  • Its 15 July 2026 licensing disclosure says it is authorised by the MFSA for investment services (Investment Services Act) and for e-money and payment services (Financial Institutions Act).
  • Registered office: 171 Old Bakery Street, Valletta; business address Avenue 77, Mriehel.
  • The entity came with the Alpha Group acquisition (c. $2.4bn).
  • Corpay Cross-Border's published office list has no Malta office. Corpayss Limited (Sliema) appears unrelated.

Licence wording is Corpay's own disclosure; the MFSA register itself was not checked.
