Overview

What GMO Payment Gateway does

GMO Payment Gateway began as a payment agent between merchants and card issuers, handling authorisation, settlement aggregation and data transmission. It has grown into Japan's e-commerce payment processor, with a second leg in in-store payments through the stera terminal platform and deferred payment services.

In January 2025 it acquired Enpay (now GMO Enpay), which collects school and nursery fees, as it pushes beyond e-commerce into everyday-life payments.
