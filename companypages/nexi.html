Overview

What Nexi Group does

Nexi serves merchants, banks and corporates with acquiring, card issuing and processing, and digital banking infrastructure. Its Italian bank partnerships are a core channel, supplemented by direct sales and ISV partners.

FY2025 revenue grew 2.1% to €3,585M while underlying growth was about 6%; the group also booked a non-cash goodwill impairment of around €3.7B.

What it sells

Services

From Nexi's annual and interim reports.

Merchant Solutions

Acquiring, POS and e-commerce acceptance, integrated payments through about 500 ISV partners; €2,037.6M of revenue in FY25.

Issuing Solutions

Card issuing and processing for banks, including the international debit product in Italy.

Digital Banking Solutions

Payment infrastructure for banks, including Verification-of-Payee across Europe, real-time and account payments.

Alternative methods

Wero acquiring in Germany with Computop, WeChat Pay in Switzerland, PayPal and Klarna partnerships.
