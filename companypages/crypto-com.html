Overview

What Crypto.com does

Crypto.com runs a consumer crypto app, a trading exchange, payment cards and merchant payments, and is expanding into derivatives, prediction markets and tokenised securities.

Launched in 2016 as Monaco, the company rebranded after acquiring the Crypto.com domain and migrated its MCO token to CRO in 2020. Its funding was historically modest — roughly $13m seed plus a 2017 token sale — until July 2026, when Citadel Securities invested $400m at a $20bn valuation, the company's first institutional round. The capital is earmarked for tokenised securities, derivatives and other asset classes.

Services

Product lines

Crypto.com App

Retail buy, sell, swap and custody of crypto-assets; the core consumer product.

Exchange

Spot order-book trading, perpetual futures and trading API for active and institutional traders.

Visa cards

Prepaid (and in some markets credit) cards with tiered cashback linked to CRO staking.

Crypto.com Pay

Merchant crypto payments — e.g. Emirates flight bookings integrated in 2026.

Earn & staking

Staking and yield products; availability varies by jurisdiction (restricted for some EU residents).

Onchain wallet & Cronos

Self-custody wallet and the Cronos blockchain ecosystem around the CRO token.

Prediction markets

Event contracts, spun out as the standalone OG app in 2026; partnerships with DraftKings and FanDuel.

Institutional & custody

Custody, prime services and planned tokenised stocks and ETFs.

Relationship with Malta

EU hub in Malta

Malta is Crypto.com's regulatory base for the whole EEA.

Malta footprint

  • Foris DAX MT Limited — MiCA CASP authorised by the MFSA on 27 January 2025 for custody, crypto–fiat and crypto–crypto exchange, order execution, reception and transmission of orders, and transfers. It surrendered its earlier VFA licence as a condition of MiCA authorisation.
  • Among the first four MiCA CASPs on the MFSA register, alongside Bitpanda, OKX and ZBX.
  • Passported to 29 EEA states from 12 February 2025, including Italy and the Netherlands.
  • Foris MT Limited — MFSA-licensed EMI and card issuer.
  • Registered address: Level 7, Spinola Park, Triq Mikiel Ang Borg, St Julian's.
Relationship with Deloitte

Deloitte

No direct relationship identified

Opportunity lens (analysis, not sourced fact)

  • Two MFSA-licensed entities (CASP and EMI) carry annual audit, client-asset safeguarding and DORA ICT obligations — natural scope for Deloitte Malta assurance, subject to independence checks.
  • The $400m Citadel round and move into tokenised securities increase demand for institutional-grade controls reporting (ISAE 3402 / SOC 1–2) and reserve assurance.
