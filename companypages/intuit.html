Company profile — Finance software

Intuit

A Mountain View software company behind TurboTax, QuickBooks, Credit Karma and Mailchimp, serving consumers, small and mid-sized businesses and accountants, almost entirely in the US.

Overview

What Intuit does

Intuit describes itself as an AI-driven expert platform. Its four segments are Global Business Solutions (QuickBooks, Mailchimp, Intuit Enterprise Suite), Consumer (TurboTax), Credit Karma and ProTax.

Fiscal 2025 total net revenue was $18.8 billion, up 16%. All segments sell primarily in the US, and international revenue was about 8% of the total.

What it sells

Services

Intuit's products by segment.

QuickBooks

Accounting, payments, payroll, bill pay, business accounts and QuickBooks Capital financing.

Mailchimp

Marketing automation and customer relationship tools.

TurboTax

DIY and assisted tax preparation in the US and Canada.

Credit Karma

Free credit scores and monitoring, product recommendations, and Credit Karma Money accounts.

Intuit Enterprise Suite

AI-powered multi-entity financial management for mid-market firms.

ProTax

Lacerte, ProSeries, ProConnect, ProFile and ProTax Online for accountants.

How it earns

Revenue streams

Subscriptions and services dominate, with referral income at Credit Karma.

FY2025 segment split

  • Global Business Solutions: $11,077M (59%)
  • Consumer: $4,870M (26%)
  • Credit Karma: $2,263M (12%)
  • ProTax: $621M (3%)

How it earns

  • Subscriptions and services for QuickBooks, payroll, payments, capital, Mailchimp and TurboTax
  • Desktop software and form updates
  • Credit Karma: mainly cost-per-action referral fees, plus advertising and Credit Karma Money
