Overview

What Razorpay does

Razorpay started as a payment gateway and now includes RazorpayX business banking, lending and POS. It reports $180bn+ annualised TPV and 12 million merchants.

It completed a reverse flip to India in May 2025; the resulting one-off costs drove an FY25 loss.
